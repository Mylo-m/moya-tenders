# Business Context — MY-LO / Moya

**MY-LO (My Life Online)** — owner Kamil Meer Motala. South-African digital
agency + product studio. mylo.co.za.

**Moya** = the agentic tender desk product. Problem it solves: African SMEs
drown in 200-page tender PDFs across 15 national portals; bidding costs more
than the work. Moya finds, shreds, and auto-drafts the bid package.

**Customer-facing surface:** `mylo.co.za/moya_data/dashboard.php` (command
center) — must always be visible/working. The cyber SPA at `public_html/Moya/`
is the preferred aesthetic.

**Commercial model:** clients supply their own API/LLM key and pay providers
direct (MY-LO never carries their cost). Founding-member access via
`signup_plan.php`.
